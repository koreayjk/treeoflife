About us

A welcome table for the nations.

Tree of Life Global Missions began in Bryan, Texas with one simple idea: every student should be able to read Scripture in the language of their heart.

01Our calling

We reach national and international students, and neighbors across Texas, with the love of Jesus—placing Scripture in their hands in their own native heart languages.

02The founders

Fayez Farag & Isis Hanna

After thoughtful discussion and fervent prayer, God led us to start a ministry that makes a welcoming home for international scholars arriving in Bryan and College Station.

By meeting students in their native languages and sharing a table with them, we help every scholar feel valued and introduce them to the message of Jesus.

Meet the team Partner with us

03How we serve

Three things we hold to.

01

Heart language

Scripture is most alive in the language a person thinks and prays in, so that is the Bible we offer.

02

Hospitality

Dinners, rides, welcome packs and holidays in a family home. Friendship comes before everything else.

03

Freely given

Every Bible is a gift. Supporters make that possible, and we keep our costs low so gifts reach the field.
