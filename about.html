The Story of Founders

Fayez Farag & Isis Hanna

After thoughtful discussions and fervent prayer, God guided us to start a ministry dedicated to creating a welcoming home for international scholars arriving in Bryan & College Station.

By connecting with students in their native languages and offering table hospitality, we help every scholar feel valued and introduce them to the transforming message of Jesus.
