03Questions

Good to know.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes. Tree of Life Global Missions is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Zeffy emails an official receipt right after your gift.

What is the optional tip on the Zeffy form?

Zeffy does not charge us fees and asks donors for an optional tip instead. Selecting $0 keeps your entire gift for the ministry.

Where does my gift go?

To native-language Bibles for international students and to the campus tables, dinners and welcome packs that surround them in Bryan and College Station.

Can I give another way?

Yes, send a check to the address above or contact us and we will help.
