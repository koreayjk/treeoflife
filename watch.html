Sermon Application & Study Guide

God’s Heart for the Nations

Reflect individually or discuss in small groups to put God's Word into action this week.

Key Scripture Passage

“After this I looked, and behold, a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne and before the Lamb, clothed in white robes, with palm branches in their hands...”

Revelation 7:9–10 (ESV)

When international scholars hear and read the gospel in the language of their childhood, cultural hesitations dissolve into profound intimacy with Jesus. God has brought the nations to our campus crossroads in Texas—our calling is to welcome them with open arms, genuine friendship, and heart-language scriptures.

Step 1 • Personal Reflection

When did God’s word feel most personal to you? How does hearing or reading the gospel in your heart language change your perspective?

Step 2 • Small Group Discussion

Discuss how our community can actively welcome international scholars living around Texas A&M, Blinn College, and surrounding universities.

Step 3 • This Week’s Action Step

Identify one international classmate, neighbor, or colleague. Offer a warm greeting or invite them for coffee or Thursday dinner this week.
