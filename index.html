International Student Mission • Bryan, Texas

Placing God’s Word into hands in their native heart languages.

Every semester, thousands of international and national students come to Texas. We reach out to both, welcoming them with Christian hospitality and sharing the living truth of Jesus—especially with students from nations where scriptures are scarce.

Why Native Languages Matter

Bridging Hearts Beyond English

While students learn English for their academic pursuits, faith remains deeply personal. By providing Bibles in English, Spanish (Latin American), Spanish (European), Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Russian, Hindi, Urdu, Korean, and Italian, we give students the opportunity to encounter God’s Word in the language of their hearts and heritage.
